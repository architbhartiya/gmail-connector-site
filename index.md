# Personal Gmail Reader (Claude Connector)

A small personal tool that lets the owner's own AI assistant (Claude Code) search and read their own Gmail
and create email drafts. It runs only on the owner's Mac.

- **Who uses it:** one person, the owner (Archit Bhartiya). It is not offered to anyone else.
- **What it does:** searches and reads messages, lists labels, and creates drafts. It cannot send or delete email.
- **Where it runs:** locally on the owner's computer. No server, no website, no third-party service.
- **Google data:** see the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).
